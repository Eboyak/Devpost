Teachings

This part of the site is for learning about religions, mythologies, magick, rituals, astrology, prayers, and symbols. The goal is to be respectful and curious, not to pretend one short webpage can explain a whole living tradition.

Notes here should be checked against academic sources, primary texts, and voices from the communities being discussed.
